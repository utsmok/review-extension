AI2 Asta is recommended for academic use. It restricts outputs to the verifiable Semantic Scholar corpus, exposes its reasoning chain, and is free and mostly open-sourced — excelling in Traceability and Reliability.

About this review

TRUST is a framework for evaluating AI-based search tools.

TRUST grades information tools across five principles — Transparent, Reliable, User-centric, Soundness, and Traceable — inspired by the FAIR principles. Reviews are done in pairs, with captured evidence linked to every score, and end in a shared verdict. This page summarises one such review; the full report has the evidence and reasoning.
