The framework

Five principles. One scorecard.

TRUST turns a fuzzy question — "can I rely on this AI search tool?" — into something you can actually grade. Every review scores a tool against five plain-English principles.

TR
T
Transparent
How the tool works, where its data comes from, and its limitations — clearly documented.
RE
R
Reliable
Outputs are accurate, verifiable, consistent. Real sources, not hallucinated.
US
U
User-centric
Fit for academic use and integrates into everyday research workflows.
SE
S
Soundness
Algorithmic fairness, bias mitigation, and accuracy of rankings.
TC
T
Traceable
Every claim can be traced back to a primary source.
T·R·U·S·T — inspired by the FAIR principles for data management.
The method

How a review works

Four steps, from first click to a shareable report. Structured enough to be rigorous, fast enough to actually use.

01 / GATES

Quality gates

Four yes/no checks covering privacy, intellectual property, and accessibility. Fail a gate, the review flags it.

02 / SCORE

Score 0–3

Two questions per principle — ten in total — each graded from 0 (fails) to 3 (fully meets it).

03 / EVIDENCE

Capture evidence

Screenshots and notes grabbed in the browser, tagged to the exact question they answer.

04 / REPORT

Generate report

A standalone HTML report with a quick-read summary and click-to-zoom evidence.

What we found

Not all AI search is equal.

Our information-specialist team piloted the protocol. Here's the shape of the results — a clear spread from recommended to not recommended.

✓ Recommended
ai2 Asta

Restricts outputs to the Semantic Scholar corpus and exposes its reasoning chain. Free and mostly open-source. Excels in Traceability and Reliability.

Where it's going

The roadmap.

Now

Standardise the protocol

Refining TRUST into a standardised evaluation protocol with an interactive, integrated workflow — structured data entry plus automatic processing.

Next

A public approved-tools list

A database that powers a public-facing list of approved tools, each accompanied by user guides.

Later

Beyond search

Extend to AI features embedded in existing platforms, and integrate into institutional AI policies and initiatives.
