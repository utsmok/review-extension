A review bench for AI academic search.

TRUST is a framework and browser extension that grades AI search tools — Transparent, Reliable, User-centric, Sound, Traceable. Built by information specialists, for anyone navigating AI search.

What it does

An instrument, not an opinion.

TRUST turns gut feelings about AI search into structured, evidence-backed scores you can defend and share.

The evaluation surface

Score a tool, side by side with the tool itself.

A simulated browser on the left, the review bench on the right.

Quality gates

Four yes/no checks on privacy, intellectual property, and accessibility.

Score 0–3

Ten rubric questions across five principles, each graded fail → good.

Capture evidence

Screenshots and notes grabbed in-browser, tagged to the question they answer.

Standalone reports

Export a single HTML report — quick-read summary plus click-to-zoom evidence.

The framework

Five principles, one scorecard.

Each principle is graded on its own. Hover to light it up — that's the colour it carries through every review.

What we found

Not all AI search is equal.

Our team piloted the protocol. The verdicts spread cleanly from recommended to not recommended.

✓ Recommended
ai2 Asta

Restricts outputs to the Semantic Scholar corpus and exposes its reasoning chain. Free and mostly open-source. Excels in Traceability and Reliability.

? Needs review
Elicit

Valuable for abstract summarization, but its ranking algorithm isn't fully disclosed (limiting Transparency) and the free version is capped (affecting User-centric).

provisional · illustrative
✕ Not recommended
ChatGPT · Gemini · Perplexity

Generic LLM interfaces and general AI search are prone to citation errors and lack rigorous, verifiable academic sources — failing Reliability and Traceability.

The method

How a review flows.

01 / GATES

Quality gates

Four yes/no checks on privacy, IP, and accessibility.

02 / SCORE

Score 0–3

Ten questions — two per principle — graded fail to good.

03 / EVIDENCE

Capture

Screenshots and notes tagged to the question they answer.

04 / REPORT

Report

A standalone HTML report with summary and zoomable evidence.
