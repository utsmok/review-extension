## Why this matters
`jpeg-js` and `pngjs` are declared `devDependencies` but are dynamically imported at runtime in `lib/image-convert.ts` and ship in the extension bundle — the manifest classification is wrong (misleading for anyone auditing what the extension actually runs). Separately, `@types/node ^25` is three majors ahead of the project's declared Node target (`.nvmrc` = 22.22.3, CI runs Node 22), so TypeScript may permit Node-25 APIs that don't exist at runtime in build scripts.
